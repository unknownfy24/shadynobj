The Origin Story

About the League

It started in 2014 with a handful of degenerates, a stolen slogan, and a group chat that has not gone quiet since. What began as a way to keep in touch became a year-long war of attrition fought over waiver wire scraps and questionable start/sit decisions.

The name? Non-negotiable. It's dumb, it's ours, and it stays. Every season a new champion is crowned, a new sucker finishes dead last, and everyone in between spends the offseason convinced they got robbed.

We draft in person when we can, argue about trades that never happen, and treat Sunday RedZone like a religious obligation. This is not a league you join. It's a league you survive.

Rules & Format

// DRAFT

Snake Draft

12 teams, live snake draft. Show up late and we're autodrafting a defense in the third round for you.

// SCORING

Half PPR

0.5 per reception, 6 for all TDs, and yes, your kicker still counts. Handcuff accordingly.

// SCHEDULE

14-Week Season

Head-to-head every week, then a 4-team playoff. Weeks 15–17 decide who talks the most trash next August.

// STAKES $100

Buy-In

Pay before the draft or don't pick. Winner takes the lion's share, runner-up gets their money back, everyone else gets nothing but memories.

// GLORY 1st

The Chip

Champion takes the pot, the trophy, and 12 months of undisputed bragging rights. Defend it or shut up.

// PUNISHMENT Last

The Sacko

Finish dead last and the offseason belongs to the league. The punishment is voted on. It is never gentle. Bring a camera.
