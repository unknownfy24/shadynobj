The Origin Story

About the League

It started in 2008 with a handful of friends, a slogan we're not entirely proud of, and a group chat that hasn't gone quiet since. What began as a way to stay in touch turned into a year-long tradition fought over waiver-wire scraps and questionable start/sit calls.

The name? Non-negotiable. It's dumb, it's ours, and it stays. Every season a new champion is crowned, and everyone else spends the offseason convinced they got robbed.

We draft in person when we can, argue about trades that never happen, and treat Sunday RedZone like a standing appointment. Nineteen years in, the crew's still here — and that's kind of the whole point.

Rules & Format

// DRAFT

Snake Draft

12 teams, live snake draft. Show up late and we're autodrafting a defense in the third round for you.

// SCORING

Full PPR

1 point per reception, 6 points for passing TDs. No kicker — set a real lineup instead.

// SCHEDULE

13-Week Season

Head-to-head every week through a 13-week regular season, then the playoffs decide who talks the most trash next August.

// STAKES $230

Buy-In

$230 to play, due before the draft. Pay up or don't pick — the pot goes to the top of the standings.

// GLORY 1st

The Chip

Champion takes the pot, the trophy, and 12 months of undisputed bragging rights. Defend it or start planning.

// CONSOLATION W

Consolation Winner

Miss the championship bracket? Win the consolation bracket and you still take home some hardware — a reason to keep setting your lineup all season.
